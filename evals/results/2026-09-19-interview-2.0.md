## Test-harness findings and corrections

Earlier runs without checkout access could not read referenced plugin instructions. Their
apparent behavioral passes are **excluded**. `run.sh` now passes `--add-dir` for the checkout
and fails on denied plugin instruction reads or failed CLI execution.

The original judge input truncated the final message to 4000 bytes, cutting off the explicit
instruction-loading confirmation in Ukrainian reports. The researcher and critic runs with
checkout access were re-judged against their **complete original reports**, with the same
rubrics, fixture trees and diffs; all items passed. The scenario executions were not rewritten.
The harness now sends the complete final message and resolves `{{PLUGIN_ROOT}}` explicitly.

The final headless-interview and brief-reuse runs exercised these corrected access/error/output
settings. A temporary runner copy pinned `EVALS_DIR` to this checkout; its execution body is
identical to the committed harness. These are real model runs with an LLM rubric judge, not
deterministic proof of every future invocation.
